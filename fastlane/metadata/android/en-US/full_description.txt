Zero-Trust Time is a simple time tracker for freelancers and anyone who bills by the hour. Start a timer, sort time into projects and see where your week went, while your clients and rates stay encrypted on your phone.

WHAT IT DOES
• One-tap timer that keeps running across your devices.
• Add or edit time by hand, with tags and a billable switch.
• Projects with colours, clients, hourly rates and archive.
• Reports for a day, week, month, year or any range: totals, billable time and amounts per project.
• Export to Excel (.xlsx), LibreOffice (.ods) or CSV with real dates and numbers.
• Import from spreadsheets, Toggl Track or Clockify exports.
• A live clock in the notification while a timer runs, and a "still running?" nudge.
• Home-screen timer widget with your recent projects.

WHY ZERO-TRUST
• End-to-end encrypted: everything is encrypted on your phone before it syncs. We can't read it, and neither can anyone who breaks into the server.
• One private account for every Zero-Trust app, unlocked with your fingerprint or face.
• Works offline and syncs across your devices when you're back online.
• No ads, no trackers, no analytics, no data sold. Ever.
• Open source (AGPL-3.0), so anyone can check what the app does.
• English and Hebrew, with full right-to-left support.

Part of the Zero-Trust suite: Notes, Tasks, Calendar, Contacts, Phone, Fitness, Meds, Time and Browser. One account opens them all.
